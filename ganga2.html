Animals

Animals are living beings that come in many shapes, sizes, and types, from tiny insects to massive elephants. They inhabit diverse environments such as forests, oceans, deserts, and cities, playing important roles in ecosystems by maintaining balance through activities like pollination, hunting, and decomposition. Animals can be wild or domesticated, and they exhibit fascinating behaviors adapt to their surroundings, and form intricate social structures.
